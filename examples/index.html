skinview3d-etf demo

This page attaches skinview3d-etf to a live skinview3d viewer: pick a bundled sample skin or upload your own PNG skin (64x64, or a legacy 64x32 skin that is converted automatically). The decoder preview tab draws every prepared artifact of decodeSkin(). Uploaded files are processed in your browser and are never sent anywhere.
